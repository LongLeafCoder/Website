I’m Alex Morgan, a designer, writer, and woodworker interested in the relationship between useful things and meaningful places.

My practice moves between visual design, writing, and hands-on making. I work with patience, pay attention to materials, and try to leave each project more considered than I found it.

Outside the studio, you’ll usually find me walking somewhere new, reading old field guides, or looking for the next piece of wood with a story in it.
