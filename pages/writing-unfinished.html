A better relationship with unfinished work

Some projects need a deadline. Others need a little more time in the dark.

Unfinished work is not always a problem to solve. Sometimes it is evidence that the question is still alive.

A board can sit on the bench while its final shape becomes clearer. A paragraph can wait for the sentence that gives it a reason to exist. The pause is part of the craft, even when it looks like nothing is happening.

I am learning to leave room for that pause without mistaking it for failure.
