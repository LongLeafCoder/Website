Terms

Products are handmade in small quantities. Natural variation in color, grain, and texture is part of the character of each piece.

Orders and custom work

Product availability, pricing, and estimated timing are confirmed before an order is accepted. Custom dimensions and designs are quoted individually.

Shipping and returns

Ready-to-ship items generally leave the workshop within 3 to 5 business days. Unused standard pieces may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Custom work is non-refundable.

Questions

Contact oldsouthww1@protonmail.com before ordering if you need help choosing a piece or understanding its care.
