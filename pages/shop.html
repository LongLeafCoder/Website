03 / Questions

Good to know.

Find answers about materials, care, shipping, and choosing the right board for your kitchen.

Hand wash with warm water and mild soap, dry thoroughly, and periodically refresh the wood with food-safe board oil and wax. Never put it in the dishwasher.

End-grain construction positions the wood fibers vertically, creating a durable cutting surface that is gentler on knives and designed to stand up to daily use.
