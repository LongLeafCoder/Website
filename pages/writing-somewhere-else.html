The creative value of going somewhere else

A walk, a long drive, or a new view can loosen the grip of the obvious.

Leaving a familiar room changes the scale of your attention. A road becomes a line, a field becomes a composition, and a small detail on the side of the path becomes enough to carry home.

That is useful for making. Distance interrupts the habits that tell us what a thing is supposed to be before we have really looked at it.

I come back with fewer answers, which is usually the better material for beginning something new.
